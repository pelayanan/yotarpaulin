FAQ

Questions About Quality

Are YO TERPAL NUSANTARA RAYA tarpaulins really resistant to heavy rain?

Yes. The surface of our tarpaulins is double-laminated so rainwater flows straight to the edges without seeping through — that is why our tarpaulins are the trusted cover for materials at mining sites with high rainfall.

What does GSM mean on tarpaulins?

GSM (grams per square meter) is the unit of material weight/thickness. The higher the GSM, the thicker and stronger the tarpaulin. Our team will help you pick the most economical option for your use.

Can I order with our company logo?

Yes. Besides custom sizes and colors, logo printing is available for project and tender requirements — also great for merchandise or work-zone boundaries that need clear branding.

Is there a product warranty?

There is. Products that do not match the agreed specification will be replaced. Claims are discussed directly with our team so they resolve quickly.

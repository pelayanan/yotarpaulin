Team Recommendations

Choose by Use Case

Three main considerations before ordering — or just ask our team, free of charge.

Match the Thickness (GSM)

Merchandise covers work at 80–140 GSM; canopies and trucks at 120–200 GSM; heavy equipment and mining stockpiles are safer at 150–250 GSM. The right thickness = economical price + maximum service life.

Think About Installation

Need grommets every 1 meter? Wire rings? A seamless sleeve? Tell us your field conditions — we produce to match how your goods are mounted.

Measure the Area

Measure the length and width of the area to cover, then add edge tolerance. For large areas, 2–6 meter wide roll format speeds up installation and minimizes seepage points.

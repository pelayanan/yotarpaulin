FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for factory prices?

Factory pricing applies starting from bulk quantities (e.g. 1 full roll and up). For retail amounts, prices stay competitive — ask us on WhatsApp for the latest quote.

Can I order custom sizes and colors?

Yes. Size, color, thickness (GSM), and even logo printing can be customized. Our team will guide you to the most economical specification for your needs.

How long does delivery take outside Java?

Ready-stock items are usually shipped same day or next day. Arrival estimates depend on the destination and mode (land/sea/air) — we state the estimate from the quote onward.

Is there a product warranty?

Yes. Products that do not match the agreed specification will be replaced. Claims are discussed directly with our team so they are resolved quickly.

Can you deliver straight to a mining site?

Yes. We routinely deliver to project and mining sites, including 24/7 emergency deliveries when your operations are disrupted by weather or other urgent needs.

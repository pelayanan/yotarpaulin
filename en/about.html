Company Profile

Focused on the Product, Strong on Quality

YO TERPAL NUSANTARA RAYA is a company engaged in Producing & Supplying high-quality tarpaulins for the mining industry and large-scale infrastructure projects.

We chose to focus on one thing: tarpaulins and shade nets. With that focus, we understand the material, the process, and its failure modes more deeply than a reseller ever could. Every field complaint becomes an improvement input for the next production batch.

Our customers include mining companies, infrastructure contractors, tarpaulin distributors, farmers, and fish farmers — all get the same tightly-controlled product quality.

Yoke Albertin Sumayow Owner
ABOUT US

CV. Yo Terpal Nusantara Raya

CV. Yo Terpal Nusantara Raya is a manufacturing company producing tarpaulins and shade nets for the mining and construction sectors.

The company was founded by Yoke Albertin Sumayow on the belief that every worker in the mining and construction sectors deserves reliable protective equipment.

Our Products

  • Tarpaulins — engineered to withstand extreme weather, including the rainy season, and built to resist tearing.
  • Shade nets — available for long-term project needs with durability that holds across seasons.

All products are produced by our own factory and sold without middlemen, so pricing stays transparent and competitive.

Vision

To be Indonesia's trusted manufacturer and supplier of work-protection equipment, known for quality, honest pricing, and consistent service.

Mission

  • Guarantee product quality through inspection of every production batch.
  • Provide direct factory prices with no intermediate distribution chain.
  • Deliver responsive customer service, reachable all day long.
  • Support business growth that brings prosperity to families around the company.

Our Commitment

We are committed to keeping the trust our customers place in us. If you need a tarpaulin or shade net supplier you can rely on for the long term, our team is ready to serve your project needs.
